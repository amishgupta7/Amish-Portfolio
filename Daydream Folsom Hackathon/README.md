## Daydream Folsom - Teen-led Global Game Jam Hackathon Across 100+ cities
I organized 'Daydream Folsom' as part of the world’s largest high-school game jam, which mobilized 1,500+ students across 100+ cities globally. I pitched to local sponsors to ensure the event was 100% free and accessible for underserved students. By providing pre-event tutorials and on-site technical support, I helped beginners who had never coded before successfully build and launch their first playable games, contributing to the thousands of student-led projects created worldwide during this global event.
